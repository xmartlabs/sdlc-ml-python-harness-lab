## Hypotheses

- **H1** — opencode local pure (Qwen 35B) reaches level ≥8 at lower cost but more turns.
- **H2** — conductor GPT-5.5 + Qwen 35B reaches ≥9 with fewer turns, higher cost.
- **H3** — conductor GPT-5.5 + Qwen 8B beats 8B-alone but does not reach ≥8 (executor limit).
- **H4** — incremental prompt beats naive single-shot on all local models.
- **H5** — role aliases + low temperature reduce scope failures and long loops.
- **H6** — Nemotron 120B planner raises the Qwen 35B executor ceiling above cell A (opencode-local).
- **H7** — Nemotron planner + Qwen 9B executor reaches ≥7; planning quality compensates for weak execution.
- **H8** — all-local homogeneous conductor (H) matches opencode-local (A) in score — conductor structure adds no net gain when both roles use the same model.
- **H9** — all-Nemotron (I) is the quality ceiling and the cost ceiling; establishes whether local alternatives are worth the trade-off.
